## Reading this list

- Items 1–6, 13 are the agent/CI side: Phase 13's waves and Wave J close
  them or record exactly why they cannot close yet (items 4 and 6 are
  calendar-bound to the post-Nov-1 slot).
- Items 7–12, 14, 15 are human gates. Agents prepare every one of them to a
  checklist (runbooks, smoke pack, documented decisions owed); agents
  perform none of them.
- A readiness verdict of PASS authorises *seeking* HG-1. It is not itself
  deployment approval, and no item on this list substitutes for item 15.
